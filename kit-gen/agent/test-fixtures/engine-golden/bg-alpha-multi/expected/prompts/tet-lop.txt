## Canvas
LANDSCAPE 1536x1024 px, origin top-left: x grows right, y grows down. The artwork reaches all four edges of the frame, and this is a LAYER meant to sit over another one: save a PNG with a real alpha channel, alpha 0 on every pixel the artwork does not cover.

## Art style
flat vector, red and gold, paper-cut lanterns and apricot blossom.

## Palette
Primary #C0202A (dominant: primary actions and key surfaces), secondary #E8B923 (secondary actions); neutrals derive from them. No other hues unless the art style names them.

## Layout
3x1 grid of 512x1024 px cells, 3 elements in reading order. Each cell is full-bleed landscape scene. Keep exactly this many cells in exactly this order.
Each scene fills its own cell edge to edge and bleeds off all four sides of that cell; the only gap allowed is a thin 24px line exactly on the cell boundaries.

## Transparency
Wherever the scene shows nothing — the gaps between leaves, the sky behind a treeline, the space between foreground props — leave those pixels empty: alpha 0 in the PNG, with nothing painted there. Whatever is placed behind this layer later shows through those gaps. The scene's own body stays solid.

## Text
No letters, no digits, no words of any language anywhere in the image. Faces, plates, banners, buttons and screens stay BLANK — text is composited later in the game engine.

## Scenes
The list names WHAT each cell is; the art style above decides how it looks; the geometry rules above decide shape and placement.
1) the far parallax layer of distant hills — full-bleed scene, fills its whole cell edge to edge
2) the middle parallax layer of trees — full-bleed scene, fills its whole cell edge to edge
3) the near parallax layer of grass — full-bleed scene, fills its whole cell edge to edge

## Output
One coherent set: all 3 elements share the same style. Game-ready landscape 3:2 PNG with a real alpha channel (background="transparent").
