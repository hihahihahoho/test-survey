## Canvas
SQUARE 1254x1254 px, origin top-left: x grows right, y grows down. Background fully transparent: save a PNG with a real alpha channel, alpha 0 on every pixel that is not part of a drawn element.

## Art style
soft rounded 3D clay-like objects with gentle gradients, pastel candy palette on warm cream backgrounds, chunky friendly silhouettes that stay readable at thumbnail size, glossy top highlights and soft contact shadows, summer theme: sun-bright blues and warm sand, waves, palm leaves and ice-cream motifs.

## Palette
Primary #00502f (dominant: primary actions and key surfaces), secondary #629f0d (secondary actions); neutrals derive from them. No other hues unless the art style names them.

## Layout
2x2 grid of 627x627 px cells, 4 elements in reading order. Each cell contains ONE full-body character. Keep exactly this many cells in exactly this order.
Each cell has a box, given on that element's line below: the box is the part of the cell that may be painted.
Cells are separated by empty gutters: the outer 62 px band of every cell — everything outside that box — stays completely empty, not a leaf tip, not a glow, so neighbouring elements never meet.

## Geometry
- GEOMETRY IS STRICT. Each pose is centred in its own cell and stands upright, the full figure inside its box with a clear margin above the head and below the feet.
- Draw the character as ONE natural figure, not a rim around a flat plate: no forced border, no badge frame, no plaque.
- Hair, tail, cape and anything the character holds come to rest inside the box on that line — characters stay inside their own box, never touch each other and never touch the image edges.
- The box on each line is a hard limit.

## Transparency
The space around the characters is simply empty: alpha 0 in the PNG, with nothing painted there. Each character's own body is solid all the way through.
